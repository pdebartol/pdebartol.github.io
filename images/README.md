# Restaurant Photos

Place your downloaded restaurant photos here.

## Current Status
- Folder created ✓
- Ready for your photos!

## Quick Guide

1. Download photos from your Google Photos albums
2. Save them here with simple names (e.g., `restaurant-name.jpg`)
3. Update the `photo` field in `script.js` to point to `images/filename.jpg`

See `SETUP_IMAGES.md` in the parent folder for detailed instructions.
